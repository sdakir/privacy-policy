What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

When you first open the Application you create a local profile (such as your name and email) so the app can be personalised. This profile is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to the Service Provider; no online account or registration with the Service Provider is required.

Because this profile never leaves your device, the Service Provider cannot use it to contact you and does not send you marketing messages.


What information does the Application collect automatically?

In addition, the Application may collect certain information automatically, including, but not limited to, the type of mobile device you use, your mobile devices unique device ID, the IP address of your mobile device, your mobile operating system, the type of mobile Internet browsers you use, and information about the way you use the Application.


Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

Yes. This Application accesses your device’s precise location (via GPS and/or network) to calculate accurate Islamic prayer times and the Hijri date for your area. Your latitude and longitude are sent to the AlAdhan Prayer Times API to retrieve prayer times; no personal identifiers are transmitted. Alternatively, you may pick a location manually on the map instead of granting location access.

If you enable the optional “Automatic Location” feature (which is turned off by default), the Application also accesses your location in the background — including when the app is closed or not in use — to automatically update your prayer times, Hijri date, and home-screen widgets when you travel. This background feature is entirely optional and can be turned off at any time in the app’s Settings. Your precise location is used only to compute prayer times and related content, and is not stored on any server controlled by the Service Provider.


Does the Application use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies?

The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features.


Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

Only aggregated, anonymized data is periodically transmitted to external services to aid the Service Provider in improving the Application and their service. The Service Provider may share your information with third parties in the ways that are described in this privacy statement.


Please note that the Application utilizes third-party services that have their own Privacy Policy about handling data. Below are the links to the Privacy Policy of the third-party service providers used by the Application:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymized usage events.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports (device model, OS version, stack trace; no personal data).

Where is my data stored, and is it backed up?

All data you enter (your profile, personal events, prayer location and preferences) is stored locally on your device using the platform's native secure storage (Android DataStore / iOS UserDefaults). It is never transmitted to a server controlled by the Service Provider, because no such server exists.

The Application has automatic cloud backup explicitly disabled on both Android and iOS. This means your data is not copied to Google Drive, iCloud, or any other cloud service automatically.

If you want to keep a backup of your data, the Application provides a manual export feature (Settings → Export Backup) which creates a JSON file on your device that you can save or share wherever you choose. You remain in full control of where that file goes.
